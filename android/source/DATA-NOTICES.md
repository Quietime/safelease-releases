# Public reference data and attribution

Contains information from **CEA Salesperson Information** and **Renting Out of Flats from Jan 2021**, accessed on **23 October 2026** from [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg), made available under the [Singapore Open Data Licence, version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).

Data is provided as available. SafeLease is independent and is not endorsed by CEA, HDB, the Singapore Government, or any linked platform. The licence does not grant rights in personal data or third-party rights. Only the CEA professional registry's published professional fields are included; no private contact details are collected.

Content rows total **251,421**, excluding provenance rows. This is **not** 251,421 currently available properties. There are **141 source offer links**, none independently reconfirmed as available. An offer may be stale or inaccurate. The Riverbay post advertises two rooms; it is counted once as a source post and its displayed price is labelled “from”.

HDB history covers January 2021 to September 2026. Rents are owner-declared, indicative, and not verified by HDB. Whole-flat approvals must not be treated as room rents. The source lacks unit and transaction identifiers; identical rows can represent separate approvals. Source rows are retained with a source-row number, not relabelled as unique properties.

CEA entries are a downloaded snapshot. A name/registration match does not establish that a caller or message sender is the listed person. Use CEA's current Public Register and check the actual phone number yourself.

The SGRoomRent index retains only factual category, area, quoted monthly price, source URL and access date. Photos, descriptions, personal names and phone numbers are not republished. Reddit links are unverified community accounts and do not establish allegations or legal outcomes. Summaries are not legal advice.

Google Maps content was not copied; address-search links leave the app. Relevant Xiaohongshu, TikTok and Facebook content was not publicly accessible for this import and is not included. Roomies data was not imported because its reviewed terms prohibit scraping. This dataset does not purport to include 500 posts from these social platforms.

All row counts, source URLs, access dates, coverage limitations, database hash and source CSV hashes are retained in `app/src/main/assets/reference/catalog.json`. Data updates require rebuilding with a fresh reviewed import and publishing a new APK; no background sync is claimed.
